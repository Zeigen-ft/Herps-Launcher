These are independently authored additive native content definitions and recipes.
RLCraft's early flint-tool survival is the inspiration, not a compatibility claim.
No original RLCraft, No Tree Punching, Forge or Java code/assets are included.
Vanilla item models/textures are referenced by identifier only and require the
user's legitimately prepared Minecraft resources. No game artwork is copied.

This stage deliberately uses crafting-grid flint shards and wheat-to-fiber.
It does not implement knapping by right-clicking stone, grass-fiber drops,
blocked tree punching, thirst, temperature, skills, limb health or custom mobs.
The knife and hatchet inherit vanilla wooden sword/axe behavior respectively,
with 32/48 maximum durability and no inherited anvil repair ingredient.
Physical gameplay and multiplayer acceptance must be recorded separately.
