Woodcutting Progression 0.1.0 is independently authored native RLCraft-style content.
It is not the original No Tree Punching mod or a complete RLCraft/FunCraft port.
No original Java mod code, Minecraft artwork, Forge execution or bridge is included.

This GPLv3 tag-only package opts in to the original MIT native engine extension
in native/rlcraft_woodcutting/. Its source and license are included in this
candidate. The Horizon MIT license is retained in licenses/Horizon-LICENSE.txt.
An older client cannot provide these interactions. The unchanged Flint Survival
Starter 0.1.0 supplies the existing hatchet; Flint Gathering supplies the next
starter route. Vanilla leaves/dead bushes remain available for sticks; this
stage does not rewrite their drops, axe wear, recipes or world serialization.

Install only through its dedicated card, OFF initially, before creating worlds
in an explicitly selected fresh disposable profile. Enable the starter and
gathering first. Real Survival progression and physical gameplay remain NOT RUN.
