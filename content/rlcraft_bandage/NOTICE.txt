These are independently authored additive native content definitions and a recipe.
No original RLCraft, First Aid, Forge or Java code/assets are included or executed.
This is an ordinary whole-player Regeneration consumable, not First Aid's limb
health, wounds, damage model, healing selection or a complete modpack port.
The paper model/texture is referenced by identifier only and requires the user's
legitimately prepared Minecraft resources. No game artwork is copied.

Three Plant Fibers in separate crafting slots plus one string make one bandage.
Hold use for two seconds: successful completion grants Regeneration I for five
seconds and uses one bandage in Survival. Interrupting use follows the engine's
existing consumable behavior. It supplies no food and can be used at full hunger
or health, where it may be wasted. Repeated use follows vanilla effect refreshing,
not stacking, and Creative retains items. All real gameplay remains NOT RUN.
